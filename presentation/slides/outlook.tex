\section*{Outlook}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Outlook}

    \begin{itemize}
        \item<1-> \emph{Closing the gap.} Settle the conjectured impossibility of robust $(n;n-1)$ black-box VRF-combiners.
        \item<2-> \emph{Where the current proof breaks.} For $(2;1)$, one candidate is the trivial $\O_{uppc}$ --- its responses are predictable. For $k = n-1$ at least two candidates have unpredictable responses, and their obscure queries can interact.
        \item<3-> \emph{Other primitives.} Apply the DAG-combiner framework to other public-key primitives with verifiability.
        \item<4-> \emph{Beyond black-box.} Non-black-box constructions sidestep the impossibility --- characterize what extra structure is required.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
