
\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

We introduced robust black-box DAG combiners that make the structure of robust black-box combiners 
more explicit while capturing the same set of constructions. Subsequently we answered the question 
whether there exist robust $(2;1)$ black-box VRF-combiner negatively.
The result implies an impossibility result on the family of robust $(n;\lfloor n/2\rfloor)$ black-box VRF-combiners 
placing a lower bound on the number of secure instances needed to combine VRFs in a robust way.

On the other hand we show that if we strengthen the assumptions on the candidate schemes, specifically
by requiring all of them to be uniquely provable and perfectly correct, then VRF admit simple combiner
similar to the known PRF combiners.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
While we strongly believe robust $(n;n-1)$ black-box VRF-combiner to be impossible, extending the proof 
for the impossibility of robust $(2;1)$ black-box VRF-combiners to the more general case seems difficult.
We pose this question as a conjecture.

Additionally, it is natural to think about weaker relaxations of robust black-box VRF-combiners that circumvent the impossibility 
result than the one we presented. For instance, it is interesting to study the possibility of combining VRFs if the combiner only needs to satisfy statistical correctness.

Finally, even for the strong relaxation of VRF-combiners that admit simple XOR combiner it is intersting to study 
relaxed robust black-box combiners for more advanced notions of VRFs such as 
constrainable VRFs \cite{}, key-homomorphic VRFs \cite{},
aggregable VRFs \cite{} and unbiasable VRFs \cite{}.
