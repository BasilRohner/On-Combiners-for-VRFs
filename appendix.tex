It is sufficient to find a $(1;2)$-robust black-box combiner for VRF where all candidates are uniquely provable. Given a combiner we can then construct a $(1;n)$-robust black-box combiner for VRF by nesting the combiners iteratively. A construction for the combiner is given in \cref{fig:construction1}. The key generation works by querying both candidates on the given security parameter and grouping the secret and public keys respectively. Evaluation again queries both candidate schemes on the input with their respective secret key and returns the xor of the outputs while the the individual outputs and proofs consistute the new proof. The verification algorithm checks whether the image $y$ is the result of xoring the individual outputs of the candidates and whether the individual images $y_1, y_2$ verify with respect to the verification key the proof and the pre-image.
